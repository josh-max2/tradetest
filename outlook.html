Capacity — what actually caps this

Two things are measured and they do not sit together comfortably. Captured contracts scale linearly with our size across every size we have ever run (elasticity +1.01, CI [0.80, 1.24]), and availability does not bind — we take about 6% of the flow printing at our rungs (elasticity to availability +0.06, CI [−1.41, +1.39], i.e. zero). Extrapolate that law for a year and it pays $77m, which the venue cannot clear. So it stops somewhere, and where it stops is the single largest unknown in the whole page.

Who is on the other side

In a freakout market at 25¢ and above, a taker-YES trade is someone paying up to buy the tail — an insurance buyer, the flow we earn from. A taker-NO trade is someone paying up to sell it — another fader, i.e. competition. The ratio reads directly on who we trade against.
